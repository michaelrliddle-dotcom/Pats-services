Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does drywall repair cost?

It depends on the size of the area, whether the board has to be replaced or just patched, and what texture has to be matched. We look at it first, give you a written price before anything starts, and you decide from there.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. The initial consultation and estimate are free.

Can you match my existing wall texture?

Yes — knockdown, orange peel, skip trowel, and smooth. We match the pattern and the heaviness of the existing wall so the repair blends in once it's painted.

Do you paint after the repair?

We finish and texture the wall so it's ready for paint. Tell us up front if you want the area painted and we'll include it in the scope.

How long does a typical repair take?

Small patches are often a single visit. Larger areas need drying time between coats of joint compound, so most jobs run two visits over a few days.

What areas do you serve?

Torrance and the surrounding South Bay, including Redondo Beach, Gardena, Lomita, Carson, and the Palos Verdes Peninsula.
